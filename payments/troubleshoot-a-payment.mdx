Start by finding out whether money was collected. A declined card, an unfinished payment, and a successful charge that did not save to the job need different fixes.

## You are not sure whether the card was charged

1. Open the job's **Billing** tab and look for a payment matching the amount and time.
2. Open **Settings → Payments → Transactions** and find the matching transaction. Check its status and details.
3. Compare both records before trying again.

| What you find | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A successful transaction and the correct payment on the job | The payment is recorded. Check the balance; do not charge it again. |
| A successful transaction without the correct job payment | Contact support to check the missing job record. Do not make a replacement charge. |
| A declined or failed transaction | Read the reason. Resolve it with the client before making a fresh attempt. |
| A processing or unclear result | Wait for its result or ask support to check before another attempt. |
| A QuickBooks warning after the job payment saved | Check the accounting update separately. Do not repeat the payment to fix QuickBooks. |

Have the job number, amount, approximate time, and message ready when you [contact support](mailto:contact@mountmonitor.com).

## The reader will not connect

Use **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge** on a computer. Check that the S710 reader is powered on and connected to the same shop network as the device running MountMonitor.

Then follow [Troubleshoot your card reader](/payments/set-up-a-card-reader#troubleshoot-your-card-reader). It covers browser local-network permission, network-name lookup problems, and security software that can block the connection.

If you have more than one reader, check which one this device has selected. If the reader is busy, finish the other payment first. Do not assume a connection error means a previous charge failed—check Transactions if that attempt's result is unclear.

## The card is declined

Read the message shown in the payment window. Ask the client to follow the reader's prompts or use another card or payment method if necessary.

For **Manual Entry**, check the card number, expiry, and CVC in the secure card fields. Entering card details manually uses online processing rates, which differ from reader rates.

Use the form's **Cancel** option while it is available if you need to stop a reader attempt. Check the final result before starting another payment.

## Process is unavailable

Check that:

- **Payment Amount** is greater than zero and no higher than the balance allowed by the form.
- The payment method is selected.
- Required card details are complete for **Manual Entry**.
- A reader is selected if the form asks you to choose one.

For a [split payment](/payments/split-a-payment-across-jobs), also check that **Unallocated** is zero and **Paid By** is selected when required.

## The fee or amount looks wrong

Compare **Payment Amount**, **Processing Fee**, and the final total. The amount applied to the job and the amount charged can differ when a fee is added.

Check whether you are using **Card Reader** or **Manual Entry**, and review [processing-fee settings](/payments/set-up-processing-fees). For a job balance question, see [Deposits and balances](/payments/understand-deposits-and-balances).

## A split payment updated only some jobs

If **Some jobs did not record this payment** appears, note the job numbers named in the message. The combined card charge may already have happened. Check each job and Transactions, then contact support to resolve the missing portions.

If **Payments recorded, QuickBooks needs attention** appears, the job payments were recorded. Check the linked accounting records rather than taking the payment again.

## A payment plan is not collecting

Open the job's **Details** and **Billing** tabs. Check for an outstanding signature, initial payment, or saved payment method. Use **Requirements Due** to finish setup.

If an instalment failed, read the failure message and retry information. If it is **Processing**, wait for the outcome. Updating the saved method can restart collection, so check the result before taking a separate payment. See [Manage a payment plan](/payments/manage-a-payment-plan).

## The client cannot pay online

Check that MountMonitor Payments is enabled, the job has a balance, and any required signatures or payment-method setup are complete. Check the client's saved contact details if they did not receive the reminder.

See [Request an online payment](/payments/request-an-online-payment). If checkout failed or its outcome is unclear, check Transactions before asking the client to try again.

## A payment or refund is missing from the bank

A successful client payment and a bank payout are different records. Use [Transactions and payouts](/payments/view-transactions-and-payouts) to check the payout's destination, status, and timeline.

For a client waiting on returned money, check the payment's refund history and [refund guidance](/payments/refund-a-payment). Their bank controls when the refund appears.

## Still stuck?

[Contact MountMonitor support](mailto:contact@mountmonitor.com) with the job number, amount, approximate time, device/browser, and the exact error or a screenshot. Let us know whether the job, Transactions, or the reader showed success. We can help you confirm what happened and what to do next.
