From the first deposit to the final balance, keep your payments together on the job. You can log payments you have already received, such as cash or a bank transfer, or use MountMonitor Payments to collect payments directly from your clients.

## MountMonitor Payments

Take card payments in person with [**MountMonitor card reader**](/payments/set-up-payments), or let clients pay online by card or bank transfer. MountMonitor collects the payment, saves it to the job, and updates its balance and payment status in one flow.

Follow [Take a payment](/payments/record-a-payment) for the steps, including manual card entry. You can also [create a payment plan](/payments/create-a-payment-plan) to collect scheduled instalments.

For quick access around the shop, [turn on the POS quick button](/payments/take-a-job-payment-with-pos). It stays within reach as you move through the app, whether you are collecting a job balance or taking a one-off sale at the counter.

## How do I see payments on a job?

Open a job from your jobs board, then select the **Billing** tab at the top of the job. You can see each payment's date, amount, and payment method, along with any processing fees or refunds. The job's totals and remaining balance are shown alongside the payments.
