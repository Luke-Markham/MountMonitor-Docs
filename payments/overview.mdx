You can log cash, bank transfers, or other payment methods, or use **MountMonitor Payments** and our card readers to take card payments in person.

## MountMonitor Payments

MountMonitor Payments lets you take card payments directly in MountMonitor. Use a card reader when the client is with you, or enter card details manually. You can collect a deposit, part payment, or final balance.

When you take a payment against a job, MountMonitor saves it to that job and updates its balance and payment status. You can review the processing fee and total before charging the card.

Start with [Set up MountMonitor Payments](/payments/set-up-payments) and [Set up a card reader](/payments/set-up-a-card-reader). Then follow [Take a payment](/payments/record-a-payment) for the card-reader demonstration, manual card entry, and other payment methods.

## Log cash or another payment you have received

You can also keep track of cash, checks, bank transfers, and other payments. Choose the method, enter the amount and date received, then select **Log Payment**. This records the money against the job and updates its balance.

Logging a payment records money already received. To collect a card payment through MountMonitor, keep **Charge Card** on and use **Card Reader** or **Manual Entry**. See [Take a payment](/payments/record-a-payment) for both routes.

## Keep payments within reach

The floating green **$** button gives you quick access to MountMonitor Payments from other screens. Use it to choose a job and take its payment, or choose **One-off** for a sale with no job.
