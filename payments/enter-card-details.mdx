Use [Take a payment → Enter card details manually](/payments/record-a-payment#enter-card-details-manually) for the secure card-entry screenshots and steps.

Manual card entry through MountMonitor Payments actually charges the card. These payments count as online payments and use online processing rates, which differ from card-reader rates.
