Your price list stores the prices for the mounts and services your shop offers, so you do not have to enter them each time you create a job.

When you choose a matching mount or service, MountMonitor fills in its price and any deposit you have set. For mounts, you can also set an estimated completion time, which MountMonitor uses to fill in an estimated completion date on the job.

<Info>If you work with multiple currencies, you can make a price list for each</Info>

## Where your prices are used

- **Jobs:** Fill in prices and optional deposits when you add matching mounts or services. Mounts can also use the estimated completion time you have set.
- **Job templates:** Price changes can update matching items in your saved templates.
- **Bookings:** Booking options use the prices saved in your price list.

For details about changing prices already in use, see [Change prices and deposits](/price-list/change-prices-and-deposits#where-changes-apply).

## Set up your price list

Your account starts with no prices. You can add them yourself, or send your existing price list to [support](mailto:contact@mountmonitor.com). We can put it into our format and upload it to your account to give you a head start.

To add prices yourself:

1. Open **Settings**, then select **Price List**.
2. Select **Add Price**.
3. Follow [Add a mount price](/price-list/add-a-mount-price) to enter a species, one or more mount types, and their prices. You can create species and mount types as you go.
4. Add extras such as habitat, plaques, or detachable antlers using [Add a service price](/price-list/add-a-service-price).

Start with the work you offer most often. You can add more prices later.
