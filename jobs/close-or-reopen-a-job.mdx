Keep current work active and close finished jobs when you want to retain them as read-only records. Closing a job keeps its details on file.

## Active and Closed

- **Active:** The job is open for work and counts towards your plan's active-job limit.
- **Closed:** The job is kept as a read-only record. Reopen it when you need to change its work details.

Your plan limits active jobs, not the total number of jobs you can keep. You can store as many jobs as you like, keeping finished jobs closed.

These status tags are separate from the [custom job tags](/jobs/organize-jobs-with-tags) you use to group work from an outfitter or company. A job has **Active** or **Closed**, alongside any custom tags you add.

## Close a job

1. Open the job and select **Edit Job**.
2. In **Tags** near the top, select **Closed**.
3. Check that **Closed** has replaced **Active**.
4. Select **Update Job**.

The job stays on file and no longer counts as active. If your board is filtered to **Active**, it will no longer appear in those results.

## Find a closed job

1. On the **Jobs** board, select the **filter icon**.
2. Under **Job Tags**, select **Closed** and deselect **Active**.
3. Select **Apply**.

You can also search by job number or client surname. See [Find and filter jobs](/jobs/find-and-filter-jobs) for combining search and filters.

## Reopen a job

1. Open the closed job and select **Edit Job**.
2. In the top **Tags** field, select **Active** to replace **Closed**.
3. Select **Update Job**.

You need room within your plan's active-job limit to reopen it. If the limit has been reached, close other finished jobs first or change your plan.

## Close several jobs

1. On the **Jobs** board, tick the jobs you have finished with.
2. Check the selected count, then select **Bulk Actions → Bulk edit**.
3. In **Tags**, select **Closed** and choose **Add Tags**.
4. Select **Update**.

To reopen several jobs, follow the same steps with **Active**. The active-job limit still applies.

## Stages and payments

A mount's [stage](/shop-setup/stages) describes its progress. Saving a stage change that moves all mounts on a job into the final completed stage can also close the job. Moving a mount back out of completion can make the job active again.

Closing a job is not a payment. Check the job's **Billing** tab for its payments and remaining balance.
