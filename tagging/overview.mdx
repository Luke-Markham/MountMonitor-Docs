Tags help you bring related clients and work together so you can find them again. Use a client tag to group people or businesses, a job tag to group whole jobs, or a mount and service tag to group individual items.

## Tag clients, jobs, mounts, and services

| Tag | Use it for | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Client tag** | A group of people or businesses | Outfitter or trade-client records. |
| **Job tag** | A group of whole jobs | Jobs brought in through Fisher Ranch or a regular outfitter. |
| **Mount or service tag** | A group of individual mounts or services | Mounts or services included in a seasonal promotion. |

Add client tags in **Edit Client**, job tags near the top of the job form, and mount or service tags inside that item's details. Client tags, job tags, and mount/service tags have separate lists. Tagging a client does not automatically tag their jobs.

### Active and Closed

Jobs also have an **Active** or **Closed** tag. These control whether the job is open for work or kept as a read-only record. Your plan limits the number of active jobs, but you can keep as many saved jobs as you like by closing finished work.

See [Active and closed jobs](/jobs/close-or-reopen-a-job) for closing, reopening, and finding those records later.

<Tip>
Before creating a tag, check the existing filters. On the Jobs board, you can combine **Species**, **Mount Type**, **Stages**, and **Created** to find work. On the Clients board, filters such as **Has Outstanding Balance** and **Minimum Job Count** can help you find a group without maintaining a tag. See [Find and filter jobs](/jobs/find-and-filter-jobs) or [Tag clients](/clients/organize-clients-with-tags#find-clients-with-a-tag).
</Tip>

## Choose tags you will reuse

Choose a label for a group you expect to find again, such as an outfitter, company, or seasonal promotion. Reuse that tag on the relevant work rather than creating a different tag for every job or animal.

Use a tag for details the existing filters do not capture, such as which mounts or services were part of a promotion or which jobs came through an outfitter.

Tags are not intended to identify individual animals. Use the [**Mount ID**](/quickstart#2-add-the-mount) for that, and **Notes** for one-off instructions.

Physical labels attached to animals or parts are covered in [printed tags](/tags-and-printing/overview).
